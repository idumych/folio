Folio is a native notebook app in the spirit of OneNote: notebooks, sections
and pages in a tree, with pages stored as plain Markdown files.

Pages link to each other with Obsidian-style [[wiki links]]. Renaming or
moving a page, section or notebook - in Folio, the file manager or a
terminal - rewrites every link that pointed to it. Also: backlinks, #tags,
to-do checkboxes, full-text search and drag-and-drop attachments.
